First 4 Weeks — Return-to-Gym Rule

Weeks 1–2: Perform 2 working sets of almost every exercise. Keep about 3–4 reps in reserve. No failure training. Optional Day 4 = walking/cardio + mobility only.

Weeks 3–4: Move the important exercises to the sets listed below. Keep 2–3 reps in reserve. Introduce the light athletic/power work.

Week 5 onward: Run the full program. Most sets should finish with around 1–3 good reps still possible. You do not need to train to failure to make progress.

Off Days

I don’t want complete inactivity except where you’re genuinely tired. Easy walking is beneficial.

A simple target: 20–40 minute walk, plus optionally cat-cow × 8, wall slides × 10, hip-flexor stretch, pec stretch, ankle mobility. About 5–10 minutes total.

Cardio Goal

Eventually aim to accumulate roughly 150+ minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, which is consistent with standard adult cardiovascular exercise recommendations.

Your gym cardio + walks can easily accomplish this. You do not have to run. Walking, incline walking, cycling, elliptical and rowing all count.

Posture + Decompression

3–5 days/week: Dead hang — 2–3 × 20–45 sec; Wall slides — 1–2 × 10; Chin tucks — 1–2 × 10–15; Hip-flexor stretch — 30–45 sec/side; Pec stretch — 30 sec/side; Thoracic extension — 8–10 reps.

Your strength training already contributes substantially through rows, face pulls, Y-raises, serratus work, lower traps, rear delts, rotator cuff, core and glutes.

Hanging and posture work may help you stand more naturally/upright and temporarily decompress your spine, but they won’t lengthen adult bones. Don’t do dead hangs to failure every day.

How to Progress

Suppose an exercise says 3 × 8–12. Start with a weight you can perform for something like 10 / 9 / 8 with clean technique. Over the following workouts perhaps 11 / 10 / 9, then 12 / 11 / 10, eventually 12 / 12 / 12.

Once you hit the top of the range with good form, increase the weight slightly and work your way back up. That’s double progression.

Rest Times

Big exercises: Squats, RDL, presses, rows, pulldowns, hip thrusts, leg press — approximately 2–3 minutes.

Smaller exercises: Curls, triceps, lateral raises, cuff work, calves, forearms — 60–90 seconds.

Athletic work: Rest enough for the next set to remain explosive.
